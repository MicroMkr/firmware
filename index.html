📻 Web Radio

Firmware Installer

① Flash the device

  • Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge
  • Plug the ESP32-S3 into USB
  • Close Arduino IDE if open — it locks the COM port

A port selection popup will appear — choose your ESP32-S3 COM port.

② Set up after flashing

1 After flashing the device will reboot and broadcast a Wi-Fi network called WebRadio-Setup. Connect your phone or laptop to it.
2 Open a browser and go to 192.168.4.1. You will see a setup page showing your Chip ID.
3 Send your Chip ID to the seller to receive your Activation Code.
4 Enter your Wi-Fi network name, password, and Activation Code on the setup page and click Activate & Install Firmware.
5 The device connects to your Wi-Fi, downloads and installs the radio firmware automatically (~60–90 seconds). It will reboot into the radio when done.
